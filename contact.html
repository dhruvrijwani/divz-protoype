Quick answers

When will my order arrive?

Order by 1pm AEST on a weekday for same-day dispatch. Sydney and Melbourne metro can have it the same day, other capitals in 1–2 business days and regional areas in 2–5.

Can I change my card message after ordering?

Yes, until we start packing your hamper. Reply to your order confirmation email or call us and we’ll update it.

Do you deliver on weekends?

Saturday delivery is available in Sydney and Melbourne metro. Choose a Saturday date in your bag before checkout.

Can you swap an item for an allergy?

Usually, yes. Add a note in your bag or contact us before you order and we’ll suggest a swap of equal value.
