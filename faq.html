Shipping and delivery

How much is delivery?
  • Standard tracked delivery (2–5 business days): $12, or complimentary on orders over $150.
  • Express (1–2 business days): $18.
  • Same-day courier in Sydney and Melbourne metro: $25, order by 1pm AEST.
Can I choose a delivery date?

Yes. Pick a date in your bag before checkout and we’ll dispatch your hamper to arrive that day. Christmas orders dispatch from 1 December.

Can I send hampers to several addresses?

For up to five addresses, place separate orders. For more, send us a spreadsheet through our corporate enquiry form and we’ll handle the whole list.

What if nobody is home?

Add delivery instructions at checkout. If the courier can’t leave it safely, they’ll take it to the nearest post office and text the recipient.

Orders and changes

Can I change my order?

Yes, until we start packing it. Call us or reply to your confirmation email with the change.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes. Digital gift cards from $50 are emailed instantly and never expire.

Can I pay later?

Choose Afterpay or Zip at checkout to pay in instalments.

Hampers and cards

Who writes the card?

Someone in our studio, in ink, with exactly the words you give us. Up to 250 characters. We never include prices in the box.

Do you ever substitute items?

Only when a maker runs out, and only with something of equal or greater value. We’ll email you first.

Do hampers with alcohol need ID?

Yes. The recipient must be 18 or over and the courier may ask for ID. Most hampers have a non-alcoholic option.

Returns and refunds

What if something arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 48 hours and we’ll replace the item or refund it, whichever you prefer.

Can I return a hamper?

Because hampers contain food, we can’t take them back for change of mind. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law are not affected.
