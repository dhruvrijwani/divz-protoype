Brand

The occasion leads. Everything else is quiet.

House of Zariva is built on our own Shopify theme, Intellect, in a Zariva theme style. The client’s colours and mark; Intellect’s type, sections and motion. White, sharp, and short.

One act of sending, from the homepage to checkout.

The calendar runs the site

Occasions are data: a name, a date, an order-by date and a photograph. The banners, the occasion list, the announcement and the order-by dates update themselves.

Say the date

“Arrives before Diwali”, “Order by Wednesday 4 November”. A gift’s one job is to arrive on time.

Gift when wanted

The note is never forced. Switch on Make it a gift and the hamper is wrapped on screen, with a note, a day and a hidden price.

Intellect type, not a library font.

Archivo, built into Intellect: one variable font with adjustable width. For Zariva: headings at 104% width and weight 520, body at 400, small capital labels. Emphasised words turn plum; no italics, no script fonts.

The arch and the diamond.

The client’s mark sits above the name, which is set in Intellect type, wide-spaced. It is the only ornament on the site. Corners are square, lines are 1px, there are no shadows.

Make it a gift: Intellect’s Gift Mode.

Off by default
A product page is short: name, price, options on one line, Add to bag.
Switched on
The photograph is wrapped in the chosen paper, with ribbon, bow and a tag with their name. Then the note (with occasion starters), an arrival day, and hiding the price.
In Shopify
The note is saved as line properties, the wrap is its own product line, the day and the packing slip are cart attributes. No app.

Warm, plain and certain.

Do
“Order by 1pm for same-day dispatch.” Short sentences. Name the occasion. Give the date.
Don’t
Hype, exclamation marks, countdown clocks. Even the sale says “Five days. Fifteen percent. No countdown clocks.”
