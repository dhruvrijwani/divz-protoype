Questions

What we need from Zariva, in the order it blocks the build.

Everything marked “Needs input” in the prototype is here as a question. The prototype uses a sensible assumption for each, shown in brackets.

Before we start.

The name
House of Zariva or House of Zavira? The brand guide says Zariva; some files say Zavira. (Zariva.)
Launch date and Karva Chauth
Karva Chauth is Thursday 29 October 2026, before a November launch. Launch earlier for it, or start with Diwali and plan Karva Chauth for 2027? (The prototype shows it, so the calendar can be seen working.)
The calendar
Which occasions, and their 2026 and 2027 dates and order-by dates. (Karva Chauth, Diwali, The Quiet Week, Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day, Father’s Day; sample dates.)
Black Friday
Is a quiet 15% week, once a year, right for the brand, and on which hampers? (Five days, eight hampers.)

Products and delivery.

The catalogue
Hampers, sizes, prices, contents and makers. (40 sample hampers from the first prototype, plus four Karva Chauth hampers.)
Studio location
Where hampers are packed, for delivery times, click and collect and the Acknowledgement of Country. (Alexandria, Sydney; Gadigal land.)
Delivery
Cut-off time, rates, free-delivery threshold, and same-day areas. (1pm cut-off; $12, $18, $25; free over $150; Sydney and Melbourne metro.)
Alcohol
Will any hampers contain alcohol? That needs a liquor licence and age checks on delivery. (Some do, each with an alcohol-free choice.)
Corporate
Pricing tiers and minimums, logo ribbons, lead times. (5% from 10, 10% from 50, quote from 200.)

Brand and content.

Photography
When can the hampers be shot, and the occasions? Every image in the prototype is stock.
Unboxing clips
Two or three vertical clips to launch Loupe with. (Stock clips and sample reviews on three hampers.)
The founder’s story
In their own words, for the studio page. (A draft in the studio’s voice.)
Contact
WhatsApp Business number, phone, email domain, ABN. (Placeholders.)
Handwriting
Will cards really be handwritten at volume, for example in December? The site promises it.
