Build plan

Intellect does almost all of it. Three pieces are new.

The store is built on our own Intellect theme with a Zariva theme style. Most of what the prototype shows is an existing Intellect section or setting. The parts marked custom are new code, written once and kept in Zariva’s copy. Shopify’s checkout, payments and apps stay exactly as Shopify runs them.

Store

Never touched
Checkout content, payment flows, Shopify’s cart and order handling, app behaviour. The theme only uses Shopify’s standard cart properties and attributes.

The three pieces of new code.

  1. Moments. A “Moment” metaobject (name, date, order-by date, end date for sale weeks, collection, image, headline) and the Liquid that picks the next one. It fills the first slideshow banner, the announcement, the occasion list and the order-by dates.
  2. Arrival line. “Arrives before Diwali” on cards and the product page, from the hamper’s Moments, today and the cut-off.
  3. What’s inside. A product metaobject list (item, maker, region) shown in the first collapsible tab.

Set up in Shopify, no code.

Shipping
Standard ($12, free over $150) and Express ($18) in a shipping profile; Same-day ($25) as local delivery with Sydney and Melbourne metro postcodes.
Checkout branding
Logo, plum and square corners in the checkout editor. Express checkout: Shop Pay, PayPal, Google Pay, Apple Pay.
Packing slip
Edit the packing slip template so it hides prices when the order has the “Packing slip: No prices” attribute.
Volume pricing
Automatic discounts by quantity for corporate tiers (5% from 10, 10% from 50).
Free delivery
The $150 threshold in the shipping rate, matching the reward ladder in the theme.
Welcome code
WELCOME10, sent by the sign-up email.
Customer accounts
Shopify’s new customer accounts, with a one-time code.

The limits we build inside.

From our audit of how themes meet Shopify. Each of these is handled in the design, not worked around.

One address per order
Shopify ships each order to one address. The bag says so when it holds gifts for two people, and the concierge handles multi-address orders from a list.
Checkout content
Only branding changes without Shopify Plus. So the card, the date and the packing-slip choice are all set before checkout, and checkout simply shows them.
Line properties and attributes
The card goes on each line as line item properties (To, Message, From); the arrival day and packing-slip choice are cart attributes. Both are standard Shopify, visible on the order, and work with any app.
Forms
Shopify’s contact form can’t take files, so logos and address lists come by email after the reply.
Delivery dates
The theme works out dates; Shopify doesn’t enforce them. The studio dispatches from the date on the order. A delivery-date app can add courier-level checks later if needed.
Apps and subscriptions
Intellect sends the whole product form, keeps hidden properties out of the bag, and accepts app blocks on the product, cart, article and page templates.

Two apps, both common.

Reviews
Judge.me (free plan) or Okendo. Fills the standard rating fields the theme and Loupe already read.
Afterpay
The payment method, and its on-site messaging line on product and bag.
Not needed
Bundles (Intellect build-a-box), gift messages (Gift Mode), WhatsApp (a link), video cards (Loupe is in the theme), popups (Intellect).

Sequence.

  1. Week 1. Store set-up, the Zariva style, Moments metaobject, products and metafields from the client’s catalogue.
  2. Week 2. Homepage, collections, product page with Gift Mode, bag.
  3. Week 3. Build your own, corporate, content pages, journal; shipping, checkout branding, packing slip.
  4. Week 4. Real photography and clips, Loupe, reviews, test orders on every delivery method, launch.
